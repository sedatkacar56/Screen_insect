Screen Insect

Bugs that walk around on top of your real Windows desktop. Your other apps keep working underneath, because clicks pass through the empty areas.

Get the files on GitHub Version history

Play it here

This is a browser version of v7 (the desktop game you download below). Move the mouse to aim, click to attack. You need a mouse; it does not work on phones.

Kills: 0

Keys: 1 2 3 4 or Q / F9 switch weapon. With the gun he stands still and aims; double-click to make him walk or run. Hits only land on bugs in front of him.

Play on your desktop

  1. Install Python 3 and tick "Add python.exe to PATH". Windows only.
  2. Download a .pyw file from the repo. The latest is desktop_bug_gun_v7.pyw.
  3. Double-click it. No console window opens. Bugs appear after 10 seconds.
  4. Press Ctrl + Shift + Q to quit at any time.

Sword

Fast, medium damage, widest reach.

Hammer

Slow and heavy. Knocks bugs back and stuns them.

Fists

Weak but quick. Good for small bugs.

Gun

Limited ammo: 8 bullets, one returns every 4 seconds. He stands still and aims.

A hit only lands on a bug in front of the man. Bugs beside or behind him are not hurt. Small bugs run from him; big ones attack him and bite harder the bigger they are. At zero energy he is knocked out for 6 seconds, then wakes with half energy.

Version history

v1 and v2: beetle and spider

The first prank. A beetle and a spider wander, twitch their legs and turn toward new spots. The spider chases the beetle and holds it for about 4 seconds. Click to squash; the bug comes back after 25 seconds. Bugs run from the mouse, but sometimes freeze so you can hit them.

v3: house centipede

A long centipede that eats spiders and beetles. It creeps slowly while the prey is far, then rushes in, grabs it and carries it off. The others run from it.

v4: many prey, few predators

Reworked to hold several bugs of each kind: 6 beetles, 1 spider, 1 centipede.

v5: the big zoo

Twelve kinds: ants, flies, moths, caterpillars, ladybugs, cockroaches, plus mantis, scorpion and frog. Bugs walk on window title bars and the taskbar, leave footprints, beetles lay eggs, and a bug crosses the screen every 2 minutes. Double-click slams; F8 makes the prey swarm your cursor.

v6: the arena

Calmer screen: bugs spawn slowly, one at a time, and fight for real with hit points, lunges and health bars. Winners are random. Sizes are realistic (frog and scorpion are bigger than the centipede). A little man joins with a sword, hammer and fists.

v7: the gun latest

Adds a limited-ammo gun. Only mid-size and big bugs spawn, at most 5 at once, and one extra bug is allowed for every 20 seconds in which nobody dies.

Good to know

Windows only, main monitor only, and it does not appear over games in exclusive fullscreen. Your clicks still reach the window under the cursor, so fight over an empty part of the desktop. Backup way to stop it: end pythonw.exe in Task Manager.
